Study

Atopic dermatitis transcriptomic disease signature

A two-contrast random-effects meta-analysis of 39 GEO datasets covering 2,138 individuals. Code, methods, and data pointers live in this repository.

Zenodo deposit GitHub METHODS.md
39Datasets from GEO
2,138Individuals
135Gene field-defect signature

Design

Each study is placed on a 2×2 grid of AD status (AD, healthy control) by lesion status (lesional, non-lesional). Healthy controls contribute only non-lesional skin. This yields two independent gene-level effects:

βD · disease

AD non-lesional vs healthy control

Unpaired comparison in 15 datasets. Captures the field defect already present in clinically uninvolved AD skin.

βL · lesion

AD lesional vs AD non-lesional

22 datasets, 12 with paired within-patient biopsies. Linear models block for patient when possible.

Pooling

Per-dataset differential expression uses limma (microarray) or DESeq2 (RNA-seq). Identifiers are mapped to current HGNC symbols before any merge. Random-effects meta-analysis is REML via metafor. Genes in fewer than five datasets are excluded. Heterogeneity is reported with Cochran’s Q, I², τ², and 95% prediction intervals.

Causal extensions

  • Trait vs state — genes dysregulated in uninvolved skin (trait-like) versus lesion-restricted (state-like), after housekeeping filters and |log2FC| ≥ 0.5.
  • GWAS annotation — overlap with Paternoster 2015, Sliz 2022, and Weidinger 2018 catalogs, plus GTEx skin eQTL coloc flags. This is annotation, not a formal coloc or MR test.
  • Treatment directionality — whether lesion-axis genes reverse with therapy.
  • Drug repurposing — druggability plus signature reversal.

Key outputs

  • disease_biomarker_signature_final.csv — 135-gene field-defect signature
  • core_disease_signature_final.csv — consensus-ranked disease genes
  • causal_summary.csv — integrated causal evidence
  • ad_ls_decomposition_summary.csv — three-group AD/LS partition

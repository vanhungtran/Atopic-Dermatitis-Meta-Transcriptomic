Atopic dermatitis

A cross-platform meta-analysis of the AD transcriptomic disease signature

Two independent contrasts — disease (non-lesional AD vs healthy) and lesion (lesional vs non-lesional) — pooled with random-effects meta-analysis across microarray and RNA-seq studies. Trait-like field-defect genes are separated from state-like inflammatory response genes, then annotated with GWAS support and treatment-response directionality.

metafor REML limma / DESeq2 Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.20707787
Disease axisAD non-lesional vs healthy control skin
Lesion axisAD lesional vs AD non-lesional skin
